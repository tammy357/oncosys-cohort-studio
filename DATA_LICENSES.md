## Data you add yourself (pitch-your-own)
Use only public data whose license allows your use, and list it in your README with its license. **Avoid non-commercial licenses** (for example DermaMNIST or the full SLICE-3D set): you keep the rights to what you build, and those licenses would restrict it.
